## Description (up to 10,000 characters)
PAi Health keeps your medical records and day-to-day readings in one private place: your own computer.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your information is stored on this PC only, encrypted with a passphrase that only you know.
• There's no account, no cloud copy, no advertising and no analytics.
• Each family member can have a separate vault with its own passphrase.
• A tamper-evident audit log records every change and every outside AI call.

BRING IN YOUR RECORDS
• Lab reports, visit summaries, discharge papers, scanned faxes and photos (PDF, images, Word, Excel, OpenDocument, C-CDA).
• Watch and device exports from Apple Health, Fitbit and Garmin; Android Health Connect through the phone companion; and Bluetooth blood pressure cuffs, glucose meters and scales.
• Every value is linked to the exact line it came from. A double-check step flags anything that may have been misread, and you decide.

TRACK WHAT MATTERS
• Blood pressure, glucose and weight, with clear thresholds based on published home-monitoring guidance.
• Medication schedules with on-time dose check-ins and a built-in "not sure if you took it?" safety line.
• Change windows: try one change for 14 days and see what your own readings say.
• Reminders of which routine labs apply to you by age and medications.

OPTIONAL AI, ON YOUR TERMS
• Private AI that runs on this PC. Recommended models are chosen to fit your hardware, and nothing leaves the machine.
• Or your own API key for NVIDIA Nemotron, OpenAI GPT or Anthropic Claude. You'll see a clear warning and choose exactly what may be sent.
• AI only words things and double-checks reading. It never decides a status, an alert or a suggestion.

LITERATURE, NOT GUESSWORK
• Findings link to published research from the U.S. National Library of Medicine's PubMed. Citations only; no abstracts.

OPTIONAL PHONE COMPANION
• The PAi Health Companion for Android syncs your watch data over your home Wi-Fi, encrypted, and gives dose check-ins on your phone even when the PC is off. No internet server is involved.

IMPORTANT
PAi Health is not a medical device. It organizes your records and points out things to discuss with your clinician. It doesn't diagnose, treat or replace medical advice. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from this PC. Use Settings > About and backups to make an encrypted backup first.

This product uses publicly available data from the U.S. National Library of Medicine (NLM), National Institutes of Health, Department of Health and Human Services; NLM is not responsible for the product and does not endorse or recommend this or any other product.
